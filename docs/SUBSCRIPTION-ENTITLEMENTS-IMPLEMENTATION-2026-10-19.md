# Subscription entitlements and administrator billing

## Delivered behavior

Essentials includes 100 non-archived members, two staff login accounts and one location (including HQ), at the existing ₦10,000/month or ₦108,000/year. Community remains 300/5/1 at ₦20,000/month or ₦216,000/year; Network remains 1,500/15/3 at ₦40,000/month or ₦432,000/year. Enterprise limits must be explicitly configured in the trusted subscription record's `enterprise_limits` JSON (`members`, `staff`, `locations`, nonnegative integers). Missing Enterprise values grant zero capacity, never unlimited capacity. Enterprise self-service payment remains unavailable; prices and service terms are negotiated.

Member records have no login role. Enabled non-archived profiles with staff roles consume a seat once, regardless of their number of roles. Invitations reserve a seat before Supabase network I/O, release the reservation when provisioning finishes, and expire after 15 minutes. Pending invitations count towards the allowance. Deactivation/archive releases a slot; restoration/reactivation rechecks it. Member status changes alone do not release capacity. Visitors consume a member slot only on conversion.

## Enforcement

- PostgreSQL capacity triggers serialize increases on the church row, shared with the billing ledger. All branches share the allowance. Inserts, imports, restores, offline sync and administrative scripts cannot independently exceed capacity. Existing over-limit records remain; reductions and ordinary edits are allowed.
- The existing permission and tenant/branch guards remain. A separate feature guard enforces plan access for new paid-feature writes. Read access to stored records/exports survives downgrades when the user's existing permissions permit it. Derived analytics/report generation still requires its paid feature. Archiving/deleting retained data is allowed.
- SQL feature checks protect new persisted paid-feature records, including public form submissions. External provider account resolution rechecks the active subscription before delivery/charges, including worker jobs. Platform authentication email is separate. Paid ticket checkout checks Network eligibility before contacting a merchant.
- Already accepted financial facts are preserved: settlement updates remain possible after downgrade/expiry; verified paid event registrations can receive their corresponding ticket. A recurring charge accepted just before expiry can still record its financial result against the originating recorded authorization/account. Generic sync writes cannot use settlement exceptions to bypass feature eligibility.
- Quota failures become `PLAN_LIMIT_REACHED` with safe, actionable messages; unavailable features become `PLAN_FEATURE_REQUIRED`. Imports report rejected rows. Offline drafts remain queued with a rejection reason and can be retried after capacity is available.

## Administrator experience

`/subscription` is the dedicated Plan & Billing screen, linked from the header/sidebar account menus. Only authenticated `church_admin` or `super_admin` profiles with billing permissions may request billing status, quotes, checkout, verification or scheduling. Ordinary staff see a contact-administrator message and do not fetch billing data. Custom role or HQ overrides cannot grant these screens.

The page shows current plan/state, manual renewal deadline, member/staff/location usage bars, reserved invitation count, comparison cards, server-calculated payment quotes, pending-checkout recovery and the latest 50 payment records. Administrators get dashboard allowance/renewal banners. Limit dialogs preserve the current form; only billing administrators see View plans. Feature navigation follows the current plan; historical records remain accessible through authorized direct routes and the historical-record action.

Allowance thresholds (80%, 90%, 100% for members/staff) create deduplicated bell notifications only for enabled, non-archived church/super administrators. Renewal reminders within seven days are generated by the existing persistent nightly worker; dashboard reminders are available independently. Bell details link to Plan & Billing. Notification keys include recipient, resource, threshold and paid-period expiry to avoid duplicate alerts. Location capacity is shown on the billing page without producing an always-full one-location banner.

## Payment lifecycle

- Immediate upgrades keep the current billing cycle and expiry. A server quote prorates the difference in plan prices over the remaining paid period, rounded up to whole naira. The immutable attempt stores its base subscription and target expiry. Access changes only after provider verification of reference, currency and amount.
- Repeated callbacks claim a reference once. A late payment for a subscription that has changed, or an upgrade paid after its original period expires, is recorded as received/requiring review and cannot overwrite current access. Administrators see the review status and payment reference.
- Downgrades are preferences for the next manual renewal: no charge or data deletion occurs when scheduled. The current paid plan stays active until expiry. The lower plan is applied after its renewal payment is verified. Administrators can cancel the scheduled preference. Active subscriptions cannot renew early or switch billing cycles immediately; renew after expiry, or upgrade within the existing cycle.
- No automatic card charges, automatic refunds or automatic Enterprise grants were introduced. Tenant giving still uses the church's own merchant/provider accounts.

## Deployment

1. Inspect existing churches' paid agreements and capacity usage. Configure explicit Enterprise limits where applicable. Preserve existing records even if already over quota.
2. Apply any pending Member-role retirement migration, then `20261019140000_subscription_entitlements`. This adds subscription/payment lifecycle fields, invitation reservations, notification deduplication and database functions/triggers. It does not retroactively activate unpaid churches.
3. Deploy API, persistent queue/nightly worker and web together. Regenerate Prisma Client during the backend build. No live migration, payment or deployment was performed during implementation.
4. Verify merchant callbacks and an actual administrator upgrade on the intended environment before production acceptance. Ensure the nightly worker runs for bell renewal reminders. Monitor infrastructure limits separately from commercial allowances.

Development/demo seed data must belong to a church with an explicit adequate subscription before seeding paid features; no seed or administrator bypass silently grants a paid subscription. Existing unpaid demo churches are intentionally not grandfathered.

## Verification

Disposable PostgreSQL checks cover catalog parity, Enterprise fail-closed behavior, concurrent final-slot contention, member restoration, staff reservations/reactivation, HQ capacity, historical retention, administrator-only threshold/renewal notification deduplication and real Prisma error normalization. Production-browser fixtures cover administrator usage/upgrade review and trusted checkout, staff billing isolation, and downgrade scheduling without a charge. Providers are mocked in these checks; they are not live merchant transactions.
